###

I build the agentic and retrieval layers that sit between an LLM and a product people actually pay for. Most of my work is the unglamorous half: grounding, correctness, latency, access control. The part that decides whether a demo survives contact with a real user.

- 🔭 Currently at **LegittAI**, building an AI workflow-automation engine, a LangGraph multi-agent delegation framework, and a three-tier legal-domain RAG orchestrator over Qdrant
- 🧪 Proudest of killing a hallucination class I call **stale-narrative anchoring**, by grounding answers in authoritative data and shipping a correctness test suite
- 🎓 Final-year **B.Tech in CS and Applied Mathematics** at [IIIT Delhi](https://github.com/IIIT-Delhi)
- 🌱 Founder of **CrescoCare**, a social welfare NGO working on health camps, education drives, and livelihood support
- 💬 Ask me about multi-agent orchestration, RAG evaluation, or why deterministic routing beat an LLM router in production
